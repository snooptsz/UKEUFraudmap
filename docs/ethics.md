# Ethics

FraudMap UK+EU is designed for lawful OSINT, public-interest analysis and data-analysis portfolio work.

## Principles

- Public sources only.
- No hacked data.
- No leaked data.
- No private datasets.
- No personal targeting.
- No intrusive scanning.
- No automated reporting to police, banks or regulators.
- No naming private individuals unless already part of a public source and necessary for context.

## Responsible use

The dashboard should be used for awareness, trend analysis, research and reporting support. It should not be used to harass, expose, contact or profile victims, suspects or private individuals.

## Verification

All outputs require manual review before publication. The app uses keyword classification and public-source metadata, so false positives are possible.

## Location handling

Locations are treated as mentions only. A location mention does not prove that the fraud occurred there.

## Reporting advice

People affected by fraud should use official reporting channels, contact their bank where money is involved, preserve evidence and follow national consumer, police or cybercrime guidance.
